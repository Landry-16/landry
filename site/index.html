    Everything you reach for, one shortcut away

    Landry indexes what is on your computer and answers as you type. Results are ranked by what you actually use, at the time of day you use it.

    Launch and switch

    Apps from .desktop, Flatpak and Snap, found by initials and with typos. If the app is already open, Enter jumps to its window.

    • vsc Visual Studio Code
    • /fenetres every open window

    Find files, and inside them

    Names, recent files, bookmarks, type and date filters, and the content of your documents and projects.

    • pdf: invoice <7j
    • /contenu quote 2024

    Clipboard history

    The last 100 texts you copied, searchable, pasted back where you were. Kept in memory only; password managers are ignored.

    • /presse-papiers meeting

    Convert files

    Ctrl+K on a file: images to PNG, JPG or WebP, PDF pages to images, documents to PDF, video to MP4, WebM or GIF, audio to MP3.

    Words and characters

    Definitions and synonyms from Wiktionary, emojis and special characters searched in French or English, pasted in one keystroke.

    • /def house · /syn fast
    • /emoji heart · /car dash

    Calculate and plan

    Maths, units, currencies, time zones, working days, VAT; tasks with reminders, timers, Pomodoro, time tracking, Markdown notes.

    • 15h Paris en New York
    • minuteur 10 min pasta

    Developer tools

    Git projects with branch and state, busy ports, SSH hosts, Docker, GitHub and Vercel, and a toolbox: UUID, base64, JWT, regex, colours.

    Your desktop

    Music, volume, brightness, dark theme, do not disturb, Wi-Fi, lock, GNOME settings panels, processes, weather.

    • /spotify daft punk
    • /volume 40

    Slash commands, and aliases for whole commands

    Type / to see every command. Give any command a short name: pp = /spotify play pauses the music in one word, and sp = /spotify then sp daft punk searches Daft Punk.

    CommandDoes
    /presse-papiersClipboard history, newest first; pause or clear it
    /fenetresSwitch to an open window, most recent first
    /emoji, /carEmojis and special characters
    /def, /synDefinitions and synonyms, French or English
    /contenuFiles that contain a text
    /spotify, /mediaSearch Spotify, current track, play, pause, next
    /volume, /luminosite, /sombre, /dnd, /wifiSound, screen, theme, notifications, network
    /tuer, /reglages, /meteoProcesses, GNOME settings panels, weather

    Meet the vouivre

    A small pixel winged serpent from French folklore lives in the bar and reacts to what happens. Switch it off, or set the animation speed to 0 and it only changes pose; reduced motion is always respected.

    Install in one command

    For Ubuntu 24.04 and GNOME 46 on Wayland. The script installs what is missing, installs the package with its recommended tools, enables the GNOME extension and checks everything with landry doctor. It only asks for your password for apt.

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Landry-16/landry/dev/install.sh | bash

    From a clone, ./install.sh builds your sources, ./install.sh --release takes the latest released package, and ./install.sh --uninstall removes Landry but keeps your settings and data.

    Private by default

    Local

    Settings in ~/.config/landry, data in ~/.local/share/landry. No telemetry.

    Network only when asked

    Currency rates, GitHub, Vercel, translation, weather and dictionary are fetched only when you type them.

    Secrets stay secret

    Tokens live in the system keyring, never in a file or a backup. The clipboard history never touches the disk.
